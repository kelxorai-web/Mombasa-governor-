Mombasa, but make it better

Suleiman's big ideas for the county, pulled from his public speeches and interviews over the years.

Competence first

Hire people for skill, not politics. His main reason for running: bring competence into leadership.

Young people in leadership

He says he plans to encourage more young people to vie for political seats.

Jobs through investment

Team up with private investors on big projects so Mombasa does not depend only on money from the National Treasury. The Jomvu SEZ is his example.

Decent, affordable housing

Scale up projects like Buxton Point, with homes, playfields, clinics and a community centre built in.

Youth and women hubs

Centres that teach business skills and connect people to funding and markets. He pitched this in 2022.

A busier port and free port

In 2017 he promised a free port in Mombasa to create more jobs.

Resort city vibes

Also from 2017: make Mombasa a top resort city in East Africa again.

Listen, then lead

Open town-hall style chats ("Gumzo na Shahbal") and a shared plan for a more prosperous, inclusive and accountable Mombasa.

Note: these come from news reports and past campaigns. The official 2027 manifesto is not out yet. Swap in his team's final points before launch.

Who is Suleiman?

Suleiman Shahbal was born in Mombasa in 1960. He is the founder and chairman of GulfCap Group of Companies, with interests in finance, real estate, energy and hospitality, and a Member of the East African Legislative Assembly (EALA), where he chairs the Communication, Trade and Investment Committee.

In September 2026 he confirmed he will seek the Mombasa governorship in the 2027 General Election.

  • Education: Bachelor's in Finance, University of Illinois; International Banking studies, University of Colorado-Boulder; Global MBA, United States International University.
  • Public service: Chairman of KenTrade, appointed in 2018 and served three years.
  • Awards: Distinguished Honorary Award from KNCCI (2023); Entrepreneur of the Year, Pwani Golden Awards (2024).

Business builder

Helped launch Gulf African Bank in 2008 and grow it into one of Kenya's established banks.

Regional legislator

Serves Kenya in the East African Legislative Assembly.

Investing in Jomvu

GulfCap and DP World signed a deal for a 535-acre Special Economic Zone in Jomvu, Mombasa.

What he has built

Banking, fuel, housing, industry. The receipts:

Mombasa Special Economic Zone

A 535-acre industrial park in Jomvu, built with DP World and Mombasa County. Valued at Sh12 billion, with an estimated 7,972 direct jobs in the first phase. Signed September 8, 2026.

Gulf African Bank

Founded Kenya's first Sharia-compliant bank in 2008, opening a new segment of the industry.

Gulf Energy

Built a regional fuel company with operations across East and Southern Africa. Rubis Énergie took a controlling stake in 2019.

Buxton Point, Mombasa

A Sh6 billion housing development by GulfCap Real Estate. President Ruto launched Phase 2 in November 2023.

Starehe Point, Nairobi

An affordable housing project in Starehe, including 2,000 social housing units priced from Sh1.155 million.

GulfCap Real Estate

Named best real estate developer in Africa by the African Union for Housing Finance in 2023.

KenTrade chairman

Appointed to chair KenTrade, the agency that facilitates and promotes trade in Kenya.

Global banking career

Former Deputy General Manager at Bank Muscat and Vice President at Citibank Private Banking. Founded Gulf Cap Investments in Dubai.

Business honours

Recognised by KNCCI Mombasa at the 2023 Mombasa Business Awards for service to the local business community.

Why he is running

Suleiman says he is in this race to bring competence to politics. In his view, leaders chosen for political reasons rather than skill have held the country back, and Mombasa deserves people who can deliver.

Rooted in Mombasa

Youth skills

The Shahbal Foundation has sponsored technical training for young people at the NITA college in Mombasa.

Sport

Founded Mombasa United Football Club in 2021 and launched the Shahbal Super Cup for ward teams.

Jobs

Backs large investments, like the Jomvu economic zone, that can create employment locally.
